## Introduction

Flip7 Companion is an offline digital companion application designed to run locally on a single mobile device (phone or tablet) to assist players of the physical **Flip 7** card game. It tracks hands, calculates scores, automates action resolution, records match history, and provides an optional local TV scoreboard display.

This document sets forth both the **Terms & Conditions** governing the use of the application and the **Data Privacy Policy** explaining how game information is stored and handled.

### 3. The Companion Service & Gameplay Scope

- **Digital Scorekeeper:** The App functions strictly as an interactive scorekeeper, table manager, and rules guide. It enables a designated table host ("Banker") or players to record physical cards revealed from the deck into player hands, resolve action card interactions, compute running and final totals, and store match results.
- **Group Governance:** Players and table hosts remain fully responsible for the governance of their physical game, including the physical shuffle, deck management, dealing, resolving card disputes, and verifying that the cards recorded into the App accurately match the physical cards played on the table.

### 4. Game Modes & Editions

The App provides multiple offline game spaces and variants:
- **Classic Edition:** Implements the official scoring rules, bust triggers, and modifier mechanics for the original Flip 7 game.
- **With a Vengeance Edition:** Implements the expanded rules engine for the Vengeance edition, featuring direct player-targeting actions (Freeze, Discard, Steal, Flip Three), persistent stayed hand scoring, and dealer rotation.
- **Brutal Mode Variant:** An optional high-stakes Vengeance rules variant incorporating sub-zero scoring, negative modifier retention even when a player busts, and cutthroat Flip 7 finisher choices (+15 points for the finisher vs. -15 penalty against a designated rival).
- **Banker Mode:** A shared, single-device interface allowing one host to manage hands and turns for the entire table (2 to 18 players).
- **Demo Mode:** Temporary single-player and multi-hand practice tables for testing rules and learning card interactions without modifying saved room records.

### 5. TV Scoreboard & Local Network Streaming

- **Compatibility:** While designed to work with standard modern smart TV and tablet web browsers, compatibility with every proprietary TV browser or network topology cannot be guaranteed.

## Part II: Data Privacy Policy

### 1. Privacy Philosophy & Overview

Flip7 Companion is built with a **strict offline-first, privacy-by-design philosophy**. We believe that card game night records belong exclusively to you and your friends at the table. 

**Summary of Privacy Commitments:**
- **Zero user accounts:** No logins, passwords, emails, or phone numbers are required.
- **Zero online analytics:** No tracking libraries, telemetry beacons, crash reporters, or behavioral analytics are embedded in the App.
- **Zero advertising:** The App is completely free of ads, advertising SDKs, and marketing trackers.
- **Zero cloud storage:** All room libraries, player rosters, card histories, and settings live solely on your device.
- **Zero internet requirement:** The App runs in full offline mode without needing an active internet connection.

### 2. Information Handled by the App

The App only processes information that you explicitly enter through its user interface:
- **Player Profiles:** Nicknames or player names, selected character avatar IDs, and color badge assignments.
- **Room Configuration:** Room titles, target point goals (50–500 pts), player seat order, and selected edition/variant (Classic, Vengeance, Brutal).
- **Match Gameplay Data:** Recorded physical card IDs, turn actions (hits, stays, busts, freezes, steals, discards), round scores, and final rankings.
- **Lifetime Statistics:** Aggregated match counts, total wins, and all-time highest scores tied to local player profiles.
- **User Preferences:** Selected game edition toggle, custom target score presets, and display settings.

> [!TIP]
> **Privacy Best Practice:** Because player names are visible to anyone viewing your device or the TV Scoreboard, we recommend using simple first names or fun nicknames rather than personally identifiable information.

### 3. Zero Online Collection & No Telemetry

- The App **does not transmit** any game records, roster data, device identifiers, IP addresses, or usage patterns to any remote server or third-party service provider.
- No network requests are made to external hosts during gameplay. The bundled Android application package (APK) includes all JavaScript code, CSS styles, icons, and WebP card artwork locally.

### 4. Local Network TV Scoreboard Privacy

When you choose to activate the **Show live scores on TV** feature:
- An embedded HTTP/WebSocket service starts locally on your mobile device.
- The service listens only on your device’s local private network interface (e.g., `192.168.x.x` or mobile hotspot subnet).
- Only devices connected to your private local network that explicitly navigate to the displayed URL can view the scoreboard.
- The stream payload contains only the read-only match scoreboard (room name, player names, round numbers, scores, and status flags).
- No data is routed outside your local network boundary, and the server shuts down immediately when you stop sharing or close the room.

### 5. Storage & Retention on Device

- **Storage Location:** All data is stored in the device's sandboxed local application storage (IndexedDB and LocalStorage).
- **Retention Period:** Game data remains stored on your device indefinitely until you explicitly delete it, clear the application cache/storage, or uninstall the App.
- **Completed Room Cleanup:** Settings provides a dedicated cleanup utility that allows you to purge detailed card histories from completed matches while preserving player lifetime match counts, wins, and high scores.
- **Storage Limit Monitoring:** Settings includes an active storage meter showing exact bytes consumed against the conservative 10 MB budget.

### 6. Data Portability, Backups & Deletion

You have complete control over your data:
- **Export Backup:** You can generate and download a complete, unencrypted JSON backup file of your entire library (both Classic and Vengeance spaces) at any time from the Settings screen.
- **Restore Backup:** You can restore previously exported backup files to recover your rooms and player stats on a new or reset device.
- **Granular Deletion:** You can delete individual rooms, clear player rosters, or remove specific player profiles directly from the UI.
- **Complete Erasure:** You can erase all application data at once by selecting "Clear All Data" in Settings or by clearing data in Android App Info.

### 7. Security Considerations

- While the App does not expose your data to internet threats, data stored locally on your device shares the security profile of your device itself.
- We recommend maintaining device-level screen locks, biometric security, and using trusted, password-protected private Wi-Fi networks when casting to external displays.

### 8. Children's Privacy

Flip7 Companion is intended for general audiences and card game enthusiasts. The App does not knowingly collect, request, or store personal information from children or any other users. Because the App operates completely offline without internet transmission or backend databases, it complies with the privacy principles of COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation).

### 9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Data Privacy Policy to reflect future feature additions (such as new game variants or enhanced companion utilities). Any modifications will be incorporated into the application's in-app legal viewer (`/privacy`) and this repository documentation with a revised effective date.
